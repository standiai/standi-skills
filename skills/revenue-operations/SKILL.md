# Revenue operations

Analyze pipeline health, forecast accuracy, and go-to-market efficiency
using standard, repeatable formulas. Work from whatever deal, forecast, or
spend data you have (CRM export, spreadsheet, or finance report).

## Pipeline analysis

Look at coverage, conversion, velocity, and risk:

- **Pipeline coverage ratio** = total pipeline value / quota target. Healthy range is 3-4x.
- **Stage conversion rates** — what percentage of deals move from one stage to the next.
- **Sales velocity** = (number of opportunities × average deal size × win rate) / average sales cycle length.
- **Deal aging** — flag any deal sitting in a stage more than 2x the average cycle time for that stage.
- **Concentration risk** — flag if any single deal is more than 40% of total pipeline value.
- **Coverage gaps** — identify future quarters with insufficient pipeline to hit quota.

Weekly review checklist: is coverage above 3x quota? Which deals are aging
and need intervention? Are we over-reliant on a few large deals? Does the
stage distribution look like a healthy funnel (more deals in earlier
stages)?

## Forecast accuracy tracking

Use MAPE (Mean Absolute Percentage Error) to measure how close forecasts
have been to actuals:

```
MAPE = average of (|actual - forecast| / |actual|) × 100, across periods
```

Accuracy ratings:

| Rating | MAPE | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Excellent | <10% | Highly predictable process |
| Good | 10-15% | Reliable, minor variance |
| Fair | 15-25% | Needs process improvement |
| Poor | >25% | Significant methodology gaps |

Also check for **bias**: are forecasts consistently too high
(over-forecasting) or too low (under-forecasting)? Break accuracy down by
rep, product, or segment to find where the process needs the most work.
Run this monthly or quarterly — track whether MAPE is trending down
(improving) over time, and coach reps or segments with the highest error.

## Go-to-market efficiency

Core metrics, with formulas and healthy targets:

| Metric | Formula | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Magic Number | Net new ARR / prior period sales & marketing spend | >0.75 |
| LTV:CAC | (Avg revenue per account × gross margin / churn rate) / CAC | >3:1 |
| CAC Payback | CAC / (avg revenue per account × gross margin), in months | <18 months |
| Burn Multiple | Net burn / net new ARR | <2x |
| Rule of 40 | Revenue growth % + free cash flow margin % | >40% |
| Net Dollar Retention | (Starting ARR + expansion − contraction − churn) / starting ARR | >110% |

Run this quarterly or ahead of board prep. Use it to decide where to
allocate spend, which channels are working, and whether growth is coming
at a sustainable cost.

## Putting it together for a business review

1. Run pipeline analysis for the forward-looking view.
2. Run forecast accuracy for the backward-looking view — how well did we predict?
3. Run GTM efficiency for the cost/return view.
4. Cross-reference: does low pipeline coverage explain forecast misses? Does high CAC explain a low Magic Number?
5. Align the numbers with growth targets before presenting.
